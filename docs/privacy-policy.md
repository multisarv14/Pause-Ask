# PauseAsk Privacy Policy

*Effective date: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}*

PauseAsk ("we", "us") is an app by **{{LEGAL_ENTITY}}** that helps families stop scams. A person who receives a suspicious message can check it, and their family can reply with one tap. This policy explains, in plain language, what data we collect, why, and the rights you have, including your rights under India's **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)**.

## The short version

- We **never read your SMS, WhatsApp, contacts or photos** on our own. We only see a message when **you** share, paste, type or speak it into PauseAsk.
- Screenshots and voice **stay on your phone**. Only the extracted text is sent to us.
- Phone numbers, OTPs, account/card numbers, UPI IDs and emails are **hidden before** the text is analysed.
- Checks are **deleted automatically after 30 days**.
- There are **no ads and no third-party analytics** in PauseAsk.
- You can **delete your account and data** at any time from the app.

## What we collect

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Name, phone number | To sign you in (phone OTP) and show your family who is asking |
| Role (protected / guardian), language, large-text setting | To show the right screens in your language |
| Family circle membership | So your family can see your checks and you theirs |
| Text of the messages you check (with private details hidden), the AI result and your family's reply | To show the result, alert your family and keep your history |
| Daily check-in settings and times, SOS presses | To tell your family you're OK, or alert them |
| A notification ID for your phone | To send alerts to your phone |
| Usage counters (e.g. checks per day) | To keep the free limits fair and prevent abuse |

We do **not** collect your location, contacts, call logs, SMS inbox, photos, voice recordings or advertising IDs.

## How the AI check works

When you check a message, the hidden-details version of the text is sent to our server in India (Google Cloud / Firebase, Mumbai region **asia-south1**). Our server sends it to an AI service (currently **Google Gemini**, through Google's paid API) to get a risk assessment. The AI provider processes the text only to return that result, on our behalf, under its data-processing terms.

**The AI can be wrong.** It never says a message is "safe". Only a family member can tell you a message is OK.

## Who can see your data

- **Your family circle**: members of your circle can see your name, phone number, your checks (with private details hidden), the AI result, replies and your daily check-in status. You choose who joins your circle.
- **Service providers**: Google (Firebase and Gemini) process data for us to run the app.
- **Authorities**: only if required by Indian law.

We do **not** sell or rent your data, and we do not share it for advertising.

## How long we keep it

- **Checks**: deleted automatically **30 days** after they are created.
- **Daily usage counters**: deleted after about 2 days.
- **Your profile and circle membership**: until you delete your account.
- When you delete your account, your data is removed from our live database immediately. Copies in encrypted backups, if any, are removed within 30 days.

## Your rights (DPDP Act, 2023)

You have the right to:

- **Access**: ask what personal data we hold about you and how it is used.
- **Correction**: fix wrong data. You can change your name, language and roles in the app.
- **Erasure**: delete your data. Use **Settings → Delete account** in the app, or see [How to delete your account](/delete-account).
- **Withdraw consent**: stop using PauseAsk and delete your account at any time.
- **Grievance redressal**: complain to us and get a response.
- **Nominate** someone to exercise these rights on your behalf if you die or become unable to.

You can also complain to the **Data Protection Board of India** if you are not satisfied with our response.
