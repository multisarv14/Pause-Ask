# Delete your PauseAsk account and data

You can delete your PauseAsk account and all your data at any time.

## In the app (fastest)

1. Open **PauseAsk**.
2. Tap the **⚙️ Settings** icon at the top of the home screen.
3. Scroll down and tap **Delete account**.
4. Read what will be deleted, type **DELETE**, and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

## If you can't use the app

Email **{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}** from any address with:

- the subject **"Delete my PauseAsk account"**
- the **phone number** you used to sign up

We will confirm by SMS or call to that number (to make sure the request is really from you) and delete the account within **7 days**.

## What is deleted

- Your profile (name, phone number, language, roles, settings)
- All your checks and their results
- Your daily check-in data and usage counters
- Your membership of a family circle. If you created the circle, the member who joined earliest becomes its owner. If you were the only member, the circle is deleted.
- Your sign-in account

Replies you gave to family members' checks stay on their checks **without your name**, so their history still makes sense.

## What may be kept

- Data in encrypted backups (if any) is removed within **30 days**.
- Records we must keep by Indian law, only for as long as the law requires.
