## What PauseAsk does

PauseAsk helps you **pause before acting** on a suspicious message: it explains possible risks in simple words and lets you ask your family for help with one tap. It also offers an SOS button, a daily "I'm OK" check-in and scam alerts.

- **PauseAsk cannot detect every scam.** Scammers change their tricks all the time.
- **The AI can be wrong.** It may miss a scam or warn about a genuine message. It never says a message is "safe". Only your family can tell you a message is OK.
- PauseAsk is **not a replacement** for the police, your bank, a lawyer or emergency services. If you have lost money or shared bank details, call the **National Cyber Crime Helpline 1930** (India) and your bank **immediately**. In an emergency, call **112**.
- Always verify using a phone number you already know (for example, the number on the back of your bank card), never a number given in the message.
